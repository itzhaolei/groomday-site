Why the form is one page per pet, not per owner

Most intake forms are built around the household, so a second dog gets a second blank page and half the fields are filled in twice — or skipped. Keeping the owner block identical and the pet block separate means the interesting parts (coat, behaviour, matting, vaccine dates) stay accurate per dog, while the phone number and the payment habit only live in one place. If you run a paper system, that is a plastic sleeve per pet with the owner sheet clipped behind the first one.

How to use it on the first visit

  1. Send it before the appointment if you can. Ten minutes at the kitchen table beats ten minutes in your reception area with a nervous dog.
  2. Fill the quote and the time needed in front of the owner. The number they hear at drop-off is the number they accept at pick-up.
  3. Photograph the signed page and attach it to the pet's record. Paper in a sleeve gets wet; a photo of the page survives, and it is what you want if a charge is disputed months later.
  4. Re-check sections 3 and 6 at every visit. Vaccines expire, and a photo consent given in March is not consent in December.
  5. Take the photos the same way every time. The before-and-after photo routine sets one corner, one height and five frames, so a pair taken six weeks apart still lines up.
Where paper breaks. A signed page in a sleeve cannot tell you that the rabies shot expires next month, that this dog bit during nails last time, or which of three dogs at the same address is the one on the table. Those are the parts we put in GroomDay — vaccination expiry warnings, the bite note on the pet's timeline, and several pets grouped under one owner so the phone number is only typed once. The form above still works on its own; the app just keeps what the form learns.

Questions groomers ask about intake forms

What has to be on a dog grooming intake form?

Owner contact, the pet's identity and coat, vaccination dates, known health conditions, behaviour triggers, the service and quoted price, photo consent, and a signature. Anything that can turn into a dispute — matting, existing skin conditions, vet authorisation — needs to be on the page the owner signs, not in a text message.

Is a signed consent form legally required?

It depends on where you work; some places regulate grooming businesses and some do not. The practical reason to have one is evidence: a signed page showing the matting was recorded and the shave-down was agreed is worth more than a memory of the conversation. Check your local requirements before relying on any template, including this one.

Do I need separate permission to post grooming photos?

Yes — treat "photos for my records" and "photos on my Instagram" as two different permissions, and let the owner tick them separately. Publishing is the one people withdraw, and a single combined box means a withdrawn consent takes the record photos with it.

Can I use this form for cats or other pets?

The field structure holds; change "breed" to species and drop the coat boxes that do not apply. The consent clauses are written for dogs and should be reviewed if you groom other animals.
