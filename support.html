GroomDay Support

The day book for solo pet groomers · Version 1.0

I reinstalled and my bookings are gone

GroomDay keeps its data inside the app's private storage, so deleting the app deletes the book. This is the trade for having no account and no cloud. If you still have the old device, open Settings → Export CSV before removing it. A backup feature is planned for the next version.

Why does it say 8 clients?

The free version keeps up to 8 client records. One purchase unlocks unlimited clients, CSV export and weekly revenue reports — permanently, with no subscription.

I paid on another device

Open Settings → Restore purchase. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account used for the original purchase.

How do I change the hours on the grid?

Settings → Business hours. The timeline redraws to fit whatever range you choose.

The suggested time for a breed looks wrong

Breed durations are published averages, shown as an estimate. Your own timing always wins — edit the duration when you book, and the appointment keeps your value.

Can two groomers use it?

Not in this version. GroomDay is built for one chair. Each person can run their own copy on their own iPad.

支持（简体中文）

重装后预约都不见了

GroomDay 的数据保存在应用私有空间内，删除应用即删除整本日程。这是「没有账号、没有云端」的代价。若旧设备还在，请先执行「设置 → 导出 CSV」。备份功能已在下一版计划中。

为什么提示只能有 8 位客户

免费版最多保存 8 位客户。一次购买可永久解锁无限客户、CSV 导出与每周营收报表，不是订阅。

在另一台设备上付过款

请打开「设置 → 恢复购买」，并确认使用的是当初购买时的同一个 Apple 账户。

怎么改时间轴的营业时间

「设置 → 营业时间」，选择起止小时后时间轴会自动重绘。

品种建议的时长不准

品种时长来自公开的平均值，仅作估算参考。你自己的节奏优先——预约时直接改时长，该次预约会保留你填的值。

两位美容师能共用吗

本版不支持。GroomDay 面向单人单台，每人可在自己的 iPad 上各用一份。
