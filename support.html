I reinstalled and my bookings are gone

GroomDay keeps its data inside the app's private storage, so deleting the app deletes the book. This is the trade for having no account and no cloud. The backup exists for exactly this: open Settings → Save a backup file and keep the file wherever you trust. On the new device, put it in the GroomDay folder in the Files app and tap Settings → Restore from a backup. Backup and restore need no purchase.

What the file holds. From version 1.4 the backup carries your photos as well, so the book and the before/after images travel as one file. On 1.3 the file holds the book only and the photos stay with your device backup — if you are still on 1.3, keep an iCloud or computer backup of the phone until you update.

Will restoring delete what is already on this phone?

Yes. A restore replaces the whole book on the device, because a merge of two half-books is worse than either one. Nothing goes without a record, though: before it overwrites anything, GroomDay saves the book that is on the phone as an automatic copy, and that copy is listed at Settings → Restore from a backup under the label Auto-saved just before the restore. Pick it to go back. GroomDay keeps only the most recent automatic copy — the next restore replaces it, so the list cannot fill up with them and the photos the replaced book held are released rather than kept forever. They are ordinary backup files in the GroomDay folder; to remove one, open Files → On My iPhone → GroomDay.

If I delete a client, do the photos go too?

Yes, and there is no undo. Deleting a client removes the client, every booking that belonged to it, and the before/after photos attached to those bookings: the photos are ordinary files on this phone, so they are gone once nothing points at them any more. The confirmation says so before you tap it.

If you may want them later, save a backup first — Settings → Save a backup file carries the book and its photos in one file. To drop a single visit instead of the whole client, delete that booking, not the client.

Why does it say 8 clients?

The free version keeps up to 8 client records. One purchase unlocks unlimited clients, CSV export and weekly revenue reports — permanently, with no subscription.

I paid on another device

Open Settings → Restore purchase. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account used for the original purchase.

How do I change the hours on the grid?

Settings → Business hours. The timeline redraws to fit whatever range you choose.

The suggested time for a breed looks wrong

Breed durations are published averages, shown as an estimate. Your own timing always wins — edit the duration when you book, and the appointment keeps your value.

Can two groomers use it?

Not in this version. GroomDay is built for one chair. Each person can run their own copy on their own iPad.

重装后预约都不见了

GroomDay 的数据保存在应用私有空间内，删除应用即删除整本日程。这是「没有账号、没有云端」的代价。备份就是为这件事准备的：打开「设置 → 备份到文件」，把文件存到你放心的地方；换机后把它放进「文件」App 里的 GroomDay 文件夹，再点「设置 → 从备份恢复」。备份与恢复都免费，不需要购买。

1.4 版起照片也装在这个文件里，界面会写「此文件内含 ◯ 张照片，会和日程一起恢复」。1.3 导出的文件不含照片，照片随设备备份走——如果你还在 1.3，更新之前请保留 iCloud 或电脑备份。

恢复会把手机上现有的内容覆盖掉吗

会。恢复是整本替换，因为把两本各半的日程混在一起，比任何一本都更难用。但不会不留记录：替换之前，GroomDay 会先把手机上这本存成一份自动备份，列在同一个列表里，标签是「恢复前自动保存」，选它就能回到替换前。自动备份只留最新的一份，下次恢复时被替换掉——这样列表不会被塞满。它们就是 GroomDay 文件夹里的普通备份文件，要删可以在「文件 → 我的 iPhone → GroomDay」里删。

删掉客户，照片会一起走吗

会，而且不能撤销。删除客户会同时删掉这位客户、他名下的全部预约，以及这些预约上的美容前后对比照片——确认框里写着「美容前后对比照片也会一起删除」。如果以后可能还要用，请先备份：「设置 → 备份到文件」会把日程和照片装进一个文件。只想删掉某一次到店，就删那条预约，不要删客户。

为什么提示只能有 8 位客户

免费版最多保存 8 位客户。一次购买可永久解锁无限客户、CSV 导出与每周营收报表，不是订阅。

在另一台设备上付过款

请打开「设置 → 恢复购买」，并确认使用的是当初购买时的同一个 Apple 账户。

怎么改时间轴的营业时间

「设置 → 营业时间」，选择起止小时后时间轴会自动重绘。

品种建议的时长不准

品种时长来自公开的平均值，仅作估算参考。你自己的节奏优先——预约时直接改时长，该次预约会保留你填的值。

两位美容师能共用吗

本版不支持。GroomDay 面向单人单台，每人可在自己的 iPad 上各用一份。
