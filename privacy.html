Privacy Policy

GroomDay for iOS and iPadOS · Last updated 27 October 2026 · Version 1.0

English简体中文

The short version

GroomDay stores everything you type on your own device. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. We cannot see your clients, your prices or your schedule, because that data never reaches us.

Because we do not collect personal data, most of the obligations that usually apply to app developers (data transfers, retention schedules, processor agreements) have nothing to act on here. The sections below state that in the form regulators expect.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller for GroomDay is the independent developer publishing the app on the App Store, reachable at itzhaolei@foxmail.com. There is no company, no data protection officer and no EU representative, because no personal data is processed on our side that would require one.

2. What we collect

Nothing. Specifically, GroomDay does not collect or transmit:

  • names, phone numbers, addresses or any client or pet information you enter;
  • device identifiers, advertising identifiers (IDFA) or fingerprinting signals;
  • usage analytics, session recordings, heatmaps or crash reports tied to you;
  • location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data;
  • email addresses, passwords or any account credentials — there are no accounts.

We do not use cookies, pixels, SDKs from advertising networks, or attribution frameworks. The app ships without any third-party analytics library.

3. Where your data lives

Client names, pet names, phone numbers, notes, services, prices and appointments are written to a single file inside the app's private sandbox on your device. iOS file protection and your device passcode protect that area.

Nothing is uploaded. Deleting the app deletes that file, and we cannot recover it for you. Use Settings → Export CSV if you want a copy you control.

If you use iCloud device backup, your device backup may include the app's data. That backup belongs to your Apple ID, is encrypted by Apple, and is not accessible to us.

4. Purchases

The one-time Pro unlock is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details, no billing address and no card information — only an anonymous confirmation that a purchase occurred, which the app verifies on-device through StoreKit. Apple's handling of the transaction is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Apple separately provides us with aggregated, de-identified sales and download reports. These contain no personal data about individual users.

5. Network access

The app makes no network requests except those required by StoreKit to load the product and complete a purchase. There is no sync, no telemetry, no remote configuration and no third-party SDK.

6. Legal bases under the GDPR and UK GDPR

Where the GDPR (EU/EEA) or UK GDPR applies, processing requires a legal basis under Article 6. In practice:

ActivityLegal basis
Running the app on your device (local storage only)Art. 6(1)(b) — performance of a contract with you. Data stays on your device; we receive none.
Verifying your one-time purchaseArt. 6(1)(b) — necessary to deliver what you paid for. Apple is the controller of the payment; we only receive the entitlement.
Analytics, profiling, marketingNot carried out. No legal basis is needed because no processing occurs.

There is no automated decision-making, no profiling and no processing for direct marketing.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR, UK GDPR and similar laws you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port and object to processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing on our side to access, correct, export or erase: a request would return an empty result.

You exercise full control directly in the app: edit any record in place, or delete the app to remove everything permanently. If you still wish to make a formal request, write to itzhaolei@foxmail.com and we will confirm in writing that we hold no data about you.

Complaints. You may lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority (in the EU, the authority of your country of residence; in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office). We would appreciate the chance to answer you first.

8. California privacy rights (CCPA / CPRA)

In the past twelve months we have not sold, shared, rented or disclosed personal information, because we collect none. We do not use or disclose sensitive personal information. We do not knowingly collect information from consumers under 16. No financial incentive is offered in exchange for personal information.

Because no personal information is collected, there is no data to delete, correct, know about, or opt out of. We do not respond differently to "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" or Global Privacy Control signals, because there is no sale or sharing to stop.

9. International transfers

No personal data leaves your device, so there is no transfer to another country, no standard contractual clauses and no adequacy decision to rely on. StoreKit purchase verification is handled by Apple under its own safeguards.

10. Retention

We retain nothing. Data on your device is retained for as long as you keep it there, under your control; uninstalling the app removes it.

11. Children

GroomDay is a business tool for professional groomers and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (COPPA) or under 16 (GDPR Article 8). The app contains no advertising and no in-app purchases other than the single Pro unlock.

12. Security

Data is protected by iOS sandboxing, file protection and your device passcode. There is no server to breach and no database to leak. This also means we cannot recover your data if the device is lost — export a CSV if the record matters.

13. Changes to this policy

If a future version adds an optional feature that touches data (for example iCloud backup), this page will be updated before that version ships, with the effective date changed and the difference described in the release notes. Material changes will also be noted in the app's What's New text.

14. Contact

Questions about this policy or about data handling: itzhaolei@foxmail.com. We aim to reply within seven days.

由于我们不收集个人数据，通常约束应用开发者的多数义务（跨境传输、保存期限、数据处理协议）在本产品上没有适用对象。以下条款按监管机构期望的格式逐项说明这一点。

GroomDay 的数据控制者为在 App Store 发布本应用的独立开发者，联系方式：itzhaolei@foxmail.com。我们未设数据保护官，也未指定欧盟代表，因为我们这一侧不存在需要指定代表的个人数据处理活动。

2. 我们收集什么

不收集任何信息。具体而言，GroomDay 不收集、不传输：

  • 你录入的客户姓名、电话、地址，以及任何客户或宠物信息；
  • 设备标识、广告标识（IDFA）或任何设备指纹信息；
  • 使用统计、会话录制、热力图，或与你关联的崩溃报告；
  • 定位、通讯录、相册、麦克风或相机数据；
  • 邮箱、密码或任何账号凭据——本应用没有账号体系。

我们不使用 Cookie、像素标签、广告网络 SDK 或归因框架。应用不包含任何第三方统计库。

3. 数据存放位置

客户姓名、宠物名、电话、备注、服务项目、价格与预约记录，写入设备上应用私有沙盒内的单一文件。该区域受 iOS 文件保护与设备密码保护。

数据不会被上传。删除应用会一并删除该文件，我们无法为你恢复。如需备份，请使用「设置 → 导出 CSV」，导出的文件由你自己掌握。

若你开启了 iCloud 设备备份，备份中可能包含本应用的数据。该备份属于你的 Apple ID，由 Apple 加密，我们无法访问。

Apple 另行向我们提供聚合、去标识化的销售与下载报告，其中不含任何可识别到个人的数据。

5. 网络访问

除 StoreKit 加载商品与完成购买所必需的请求外，应用不发起任何网络请求。没有同步、没有遥测、没有远程配置、没有第三方 SDK。

本产品不进行自动化决策、不做用户画像，也不进行任何直接营销处理。

依据 GDPR、英国 GDPR 及同类法律，你享有访问、更正、删除、限制处理、数据可携与反对处理的权利，以及撤回同意的权利。由于我们不持有你的个人数据，我们这一侧没有可供访问、更正、导出或删除的内容——此类请求的结果只能为空。
