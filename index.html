Questions groomers ask

Is GroomDay free?

Downloading is free and the day book works without paying. The free version holds up to 8 clients, which is enough to run a real week. Pro is a single one-time purchase that removes the client limit and adds CSV export and weekly revenue reports.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no login and no email required. Open the app and the current day is already there.

Where is my client data stored?

On your device only. GroomDay has no cloud backend and no analytics, so nothing is uploaded. Use the CSV export to keep your own backup.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The app is fully functional with no connection — it never needs to phone home to show your day.

Does it run on iPhone and iPad?

Yes, the same app runs on both. It's laid out for reading at arm's length on the table, and it works in portrait or landscape.

Is there an Android version?

Not currently. GroomDay is built for iOS and iPadOS only.

I run a multi-chair salon with staff — is this for me?

Probably not. GroomDay is deliberately built for one-person and two-chair studios: no staff scheduling, no marketing tools, no multi-location reporting. That focus is why the day view stays readable.
